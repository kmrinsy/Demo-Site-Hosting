WHO WE ARE

Progress is meaningful when everyone moves forward.

Lahanti Last Mile Services Private Limited was incorporated in 2016 with a simple belief: progress and prosperity should be within everyone's reach.

Inspired by the idea of serving people, planet and prosperity, LLMS works to connect unserved and underserved communities with financial services and opportunities. As a Business Correspondent of ESAF Small Finance Bank, we help take banking and financial services closer to the people who need them.
